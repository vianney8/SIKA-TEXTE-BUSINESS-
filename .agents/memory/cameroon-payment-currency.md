---
name: Cameroon payment currency
description: Cameroon CFA settlement and safe recovery of historical currency-label mistakes.
---

For automatic SolvexPay payments, Cameroon CFA service prices settle in XAF, with the same numeric service amount. Other supported West African countries continue to use XOF; do not treat the two currencies as interchangeable globally.

**Why:** Historical Cameroon receipts labelled XOF caused legitimate XAF provider confirmations to remain blocked by the currency integrity check.

**How to apply:** Align the displayed quote, outbound provider currency and receipt currency. A historical pending Cameroon receipt labelled XOF may be corrected to XAF only after the provider reference, amount and other identifying fields pass validation. Never bypass a currency mismatch for another country or a non-CFA currency, and never initiate another debit just to repair the label.
